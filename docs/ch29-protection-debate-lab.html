    04

    Can an infant industry grow up?

    A new domestic industry’s average cost falls as it gains experience and scale (dynamic comparative advantage). A temporary tariff lets it survive while it learns. Set the policy and see whether the industry becomes competitive before protection ends.

    Illustrative model: world price $10; domestic cost starts at $15 and falls 6% a year with performance targets, or 2% a year without them (X-inefficiency).

    05

    “Protecting jobs”: what does each job cost?

    Protection can save jobs in the protected industry, but consumers pay higher prices and jobs may be lost elsewhere. Calculate the cost to consumers per job saved.

    06

    Trade war: retaliation in action

    Each round, choose free trade or impose tariffs. Your rival copies whatever you did last round (tit-for-tat). Tariffs win a little once, but retaliation makes both countries poorer, as in the US–China trade war from 2018.
