    03

    Free trade: exporter or importer?

    A small country is a price taker, so the world price (Pw) is a horizontal line. If Pw is above the domestic no-trade price, the country exports (Fig 27.5); if it is below, it imports (Fig 27.6). Country Z’s plum-oil market: Qd = 500 − 50P, Qs = 50P − 100 (thousand litres).

    04

    Winners and losers

    Trade raises social surplus overall, but not everyone gains. Decide whether each group gains or loses.

    05

    Absolute advantage HL

    Using the same resources, Australia can produce 6 timber or 12 cattle; Brazil 10 timber or 5 cattle (Hoang Tables 27.1–27.4). Each specialises in what it does best, then they swap timber for cattle one for one.

    06

    Comparative advantage and gains from trade HL

    Even if one country is better at everything, both gain by specialising in the good with the lower opportunity cost and trading at terms between their two opportunity costs (Fig 27.13). Load an example or type your own maximum outputs.
